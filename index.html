How it works

From a trusted source to your feed.

Videos are prepared with automated tools. Every one is checked against its source and approved by a person before it goes live.

01

Choose

A concept, a story or a news item, depending on the day.

02

Source

Facts come only from a reference: Wikipedia or the original news article.

03

Check

Every claim is verified against the source. Unsupported claims are removed.

04

Produce

Voice-over, visuals and captions are assembled into a vertical video.

05

Human approval

The account owner reviews each video before it is published on TikTok.
