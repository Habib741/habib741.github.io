How it works

From the source to your feed.

Videos are prepared with automated tools, and every one is checked against its source and approved by a person before it goes live.

01

Watch

02

A script is drafted using only facts from the original article.

03

Every claim is verified against the source. Unsupported claims are removed.

04

Voice-over, visuals and captions are assembled into a vertical video.
