Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how Beru Tech Publisher ("the App"), an internal publishing tool for the TikTok account @beru_tech, handles data.

1. Data we access

When the account owner authorizes the App through TikTok Login Kit, the App receives, from TikTok:

  • basic account information (account identifier, display name) via the user.info.basic scope;
  • access tokens that allow it to upload videos to the authorized account (video.upload, video.publish).

The App does not access followers' data, private messages, viewers' information or any other TikTok user's data.

2. How we use it

This data is used only to upload and publish videos to the @beru_tech account and to check the status of those uploads.

3. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the App's private hosting environment and are never shared. Published videos and their captions are stored on TikTok.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Video scripts are generated with third-party AI services from public news articles; no TikTok user data is sent to these services.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the App is authorized. The account owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app settings, after which the tokens stop working and are deleted.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
